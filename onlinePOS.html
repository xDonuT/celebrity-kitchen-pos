Celebrity Kitchen POS

Celebrity Kitchen POS is the official point-of-sale system developed for Celebrity Kitchen to simplify daily restaurant operations. Designed with speed, reliability, and ease of use in mind, it helps staff process orders efficiently while keeping the cashier, kitchen, and BBQ stations synchronized in real time.

The system supports order management, payment processing, menu administration, transaction history, and sales reporting through a simple and user-friendly interface.

✨ Features

  • Real-time order synchronization
  • Multi-device support
  • Cash & GCash payment processing
  • Kitchen & BBQ order management
  • Pending order tracking
  • Transaction history
  • Sales summary and CSV export
  • Menu and price management
  • Dark mode
  • Automatic cloud data synchronization
